# MOOSE MAN

> **⚠ PHOTOSENSITIVITY WARNING**
> This game contains screen glitches, flicker, a red-flashing jumpscare and
> single-frame subliminal images. **Settings → Camcorder Effects: OFF** and
> **Film Grain: OFF** tone most of it down.

*October 31, 2003. 11:58 PM. Hollins Woods, Harlan County.*

Three kids have gone missing near these woods since August. Everyone in
Millbrook has heard about the Moose Man. Nobody has ever filmed him.

You brought a flashlight and your dad's camcorder.

A first-person 3D horror game. You walk through a forest at midnight, crawl
through a storm drain into a flooded overflow sewer, and find out what has
been living down there since 1987.

Built with **Python + pygame + moderngl (OpenGL 3.3) + numpy**. There are
**no asset files**: every model, texture, and sound is generated by code when
the game starts.

## Play

Download the `.zip` from the [Releases](../../releases) page, unzip it, and run
`Moose Man.exe`. You don't need to install anything, and it updates itself.
Headphones are recommended. Play in the dark.

Or run it from source:

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
python src/game.py
```

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrows | Move |
| Mouse | Look |
| Shift | Run (limited stamina) |
| Ctrl / C | Crouch (quieter, harder to see) |
| F | Flashlight on/off |
| E | Read notes / interact. **Hold** to turn valves |
| Tab | Show objective and notes found |
| Esc | Pause |

## How to escape

- Follow the trail north to the creek and the storm drain.
- The drain leads down into Overflow System C. **He is down there.**
- Chamber C is flooded. Open the **five relief valves** hidden around the
  system to drain it, then reach the service ladder on the far side.
- Turning a valve makes noise. He hears footsteps, running, splashing, and
  valves. He sees your flashlight, and shining it at him is a bad idea.
  Crouching and switching the light off help you hide.
- After the fifth valve, he stops wandering and starts hunting you.
- If he catches you, you wake up at the sewer entrance. Valves you have
  already opened stay open.

## Chapter Two

*One day later.* You wake on a cot in a small log cabin. The stove is out and a
snowstorm is coming. Take the lantern and go out for firewood.

- Find **20 bundles of firewood** out in the snow. There are more than 20 on
  the map, scattered between the rivers, the lake, the hunting stand and the
  dock.
- **E** picks a bundle up. **E at the woodpile** on the cabin porch stacks
  everything you're carrying. Stacked wood is safe.
- **The Goat Man is out there looking for you, and he hunts light and
  movement.** Your lantern (**F**) gives you away from far off. With it out,
  he only notices you when you move. Running carries furthest, and crouching
  barely at all. **Lantern off and standing still, he can't see you**: he
  stops, stares toward where you were, looks around, and leaves. Running and
  splashing through the rivers are loud. The lake is too deep to cross.
- If he catches you, you wake on the porch. **Whatever you were carrying is
  lost** (it goes back where you found it). The woodpile keeps what you stacked.
- With 20 stacked, go inside and take the **rifle** from the rack. Hunt the
  deer grazing about 200 feet straight out in front of the cabin: **right mouse** aims through the scope, **left mouse**
  fires. Your lantern and your running will spook it.
- Butcher it, then **get back to the cabin**. Something followed you.

## Chapter Three

*The fire is going out.* A blizzard has buried everything knee-deep, and the
cabin isn't safe anymore. You take the lantern into the storm and find a cave.
When you wake, something white is crawling toward it.

- **Hide.** The Elk sees a long way through the huge caverns. It spots your
  lantern from across the mine, and it can see you in the dark **even when you
  stand still**. But it is **slower than you**, and it **can't fit into the
  narrow timbered mineshafts**. Get into one and it can only wait at the
  opening, staring in, until it gives up.
- Find and pull **three levers** in the caverns (hold **E**). Pulling one is
  loud, and it will come.
- With all three pulled, the generator starts and the lights come on. Take the
  **elevator** down from beside the mine office.
- Down there you'll find what's left of the Goat Man. Then it follows you down.
  **Find the old way out of the mine.**

Checkpoints: the start of the chapter, and the bottom of the elevator.

## Chapter Four

*You thought the Moose Man was dead.* Back in the storm drain, he walks out of
the dark and starts to **change**: bigger, heavier, both antlers grown back.
He smashes the floor and you both fall into a far bigger sewer underneath.

- Start the **three backup generators** (hold **E** on the starter cord). It's
  loud. Each one lights its own tunnels.
- With all three running, the first big gate opens onto a **huge hall**.
- Find **five fuses** in the sewers around the hall and put them in the fuse
  box beside the second gate, which is even bigger. Then go through.
- He hunts **light and movement**, and he's faster than before. He is
  **too big now for the overflow pipes**. Hide in one and he'll tear at the
  opening, then stand there, then leave.

Checkpoints: the start of the chapter, and entering the hall.

## Chapter Five

A smaller storm drain, past the second gate. Halfway down it there's a dead
hunter, and his **shotgun** (**E** to take it).

The drain opens into a derelict factory, enormous and dark. Then the Moose Man
drags in what's left of the Elk and the Goat Man and **takes them into
himself**. What stands up is **The Beast**: three times his height, the Elk's
crawling white body, the Moose Man rising out of its hump, and the Goat Man
grown out of its flank.

- **Left mouse** fires (six shells), and **R** reloads. Heads take the most
  damage. Up close does more than far away.
- You have a **health bar**. It slowly comes back if you avoid getting hit.
- It's slow, but it has a lot of ways to kill you:
  - it **slams** the floor with both hands wherever you're standing;
  - it **throws** chunks of meat that land where you're going (watch the red
    mark);
  - it **charges** in a straight line. If it hits a wall it's **stunned** and
    takes extra damage;
  - below half health it **brings the roof down**. Get off the red marks.
- Kill it and the great gate opens. Walk out into the daylight.

Checkpoints: the start of the chapter, and the start of the fight.

## Co-op

**CO-OP** on the title screen. Up to four players go through the story together.

- **HOST GAME** gives you a five-letter room code. Friends pick **JOIN GAME** and type
  it in. The host chooses the chapter and starts.
- Puzzles are shared. When anyone opens a valve, stacks wood, pulls a lever, starts a
  generator or puts in a fuse, it's done for everyone.
- Nobody moves on alone. The ladder, the cabin, the elevator, the gates and the
  exits only work once **everyone** is standing there.
- The monsters go after whoever is closest, or whoever is showing a light. If one
  catches you, you get the jumpscare and wake at the checkpoint, and everyone else
  sees it happen. The Beast gets tougher with more players.
- Pausing doesn't stop the game in co-op, and co-op doesn't change your single-player
  CONTINUE point.

It runs on Supabase Realtime: messages pass between players and nothing is
stored. The project URL and its publishable key are in `src/net.py`.

## Saving

**CONTINUE** on the title screen picks up where you left off: the start of
the chapter you were on, or its checkpoint if you reached it. Chapter 1's
checkpoint is the bottom of the storm drain, where you enter the sewers.
Chapter 2's is having all 20 bundles of firewood stacked. Chapter 3's is the
bottom of the elevator. Chapter 4's is entering the hall. Chapter 5's is the start of the fight. **PLAY** opens the chapter select. Each chapter is
locked until you beat the one before it. Progress is
saved to `%APPDATA%\MooseMan\progress.json`.

## Notes

There are **11 notes** to find: a missing-person flyer, a public works
inspection log, a sheriff's report, and the journals of Dale Ruckert, the
hunter who shot the white bull in 1987 and put on its head.

## Settings

Mouse sensitivity, invert Y, FOV, brightness, volume, render resolution
(360p / 480p / 720p / Native), film grain, camcorder effects, FPS counter,
and **Display Mode**:

- **Windowed Fullscreen** (default): a normal window that fills the screen.
  This works best for recording with OBS (use *Window Capture* with the
  *Windows 10 (1903 and up)* capture method).
- **Borderless**, **Fullscreen**, **Windowed (1280×720)**.

A Display Mode change applies the next time you start the game. Settings are
saved to `%APPDATA%\MooseMan\settings.json`.

## Updates

The game checks this repo's latest release when it starts. If there is a newer
version, it downloads it, checks its SHA-256, swaps it in and restarts. You can
turn this off in **Settings → Auto Update**, and the title screen then shows an
**UPDATE TO x.y.z** button instead. A log is written to `update.log` next to the .exe.

## Build and release

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt pyinstaller
python build_exe.py                     # build only -> dist/
python build_exe.py --bump --publish    # bump the patch version, build, publish to GitHub
python build_exe.py --minor --publish   # same, but 1.1.x -> 1.2.0
```

Publishing needs the GitHub CLI (`winget install --id GitHub.cli`, then `gh auth login`).
Each release carries three files: `Moose Man x.y.z.zip`, which is the one people
download, plus `MooseMan.exe` and `latest.json`, which the in-game updater reads.
Edit `NOTES` in `build_exe.py` before publishing. It's what the update screen shows.

## Source layout

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `src/game.py` | Main loop, game states, menus, HUD, events, ending |
| `src/engine.py` | Matrix math, mesh builder, shaders, post-processing (VHS / grain / glitch) |
| `src/world.py` | The forest, the storm drain, and the sewer grid: collision, pathfinding, geometry, props |
| `src/moose.py` | The Moose Man's model, animation and AI, plus the valve and herd-head meshes |
| `src/textures.py` | Procedural textures (noise, bricks, graffiti, signs, flyers) |
| `src/audio.py` | Synthesized sound and positional audio |
| `src/lore.py` | Every note, the intro, and the ending text |
| `src/updater.py` | Checks `latest.json`, downloads and verifies the new build, and swaps it in |
| `src/version.py` | The version number |
| `build_exe.py` | Builds the .exe, the zip and `latest.json`, and publishes the release |

`python src/game.py --autotest <folder>` renders screenshots of every area and
runs a quick AI and pathfinding self-test.
